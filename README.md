# Hi, I'm Hollie Richmond

I'm a product design leader who makes complex products easier to use, scale, and ship. I've spent 18+ years on enterprise, healthcare and AI products, including leading UI design for a regulated life sciences CRM used by close to 400 enterprise customers, and building design systems that keep large products consistent.

These days I design in Figma and in code, with AI coding agents as part of my daily workflow.
